Protostar.

Every Bright Idea Begins As A Shapeless One

This is where you shape it.

Our team is filled with exceptional experts, each with deep knowledge across a wide range of specialties. With expertise like that, the challenge is never a shortage of ideas. It is making sure that expertise reaches the full range of possibilities, rather than returning to the same familiar ground each time. Protostar combines pieces from across every domain at random, bringing new territory into view so a promising idea has somewhere to begin.

Most pairings won't be usable, and that's the point. It's combining pieces blindly, with no idea whether a given niche and consideration actually fit together, so plenty will be mismatched, redundant, or just odd. That's expected: you're a specialist panning for the occasional gem, so scrap freely and keep what sparks.

That's all it does. It sorts and combines starting points, nothing else. No prompts, no scenarios, no rubrics, no finished work, no part of the actual task. No AI is involved anywhere in it. All the real thinking, and every idea worth keeping, is yours.

Disclaimer

Before you use this: this tool is only for projects where this kind of brainstorming aid is approved, so if you're not sure it's allowed for what you're working on, check before you use it.

My additionsAdd your own domains, niches, and more, or brain-dump messy notes into organized entries.

Add your own entries to any dropdown. Pick which field you're adding to, type the entry, and click Add. Your entries appear in a "My additions" group at the top of that field's menu and are always available no matter which domain is selected. Custom entries join the random draws when the box below is checked. You can remove anything, including the built-in entries that come with the tool, using the remove section below or the × next to an entry.

Remove entries. Take out anything you don't want, including the tool's built-in entries. Pick a field and an entry, then click Remove. Removed built-ins show up below and can be restored anytime.

Brain dump. Got a wall of notes or a whole paragraph? Paste it here and click Find keywords. Protostar pulls out candidate terms for you to assign to the right dropdown (or skip). Nothing is added until you click Add all assigned.

No AI is involved. "Find keywords" is plain word-matching, not AI. It's rough on purpose: it will miss things and grab junk, so it just makes a first pass for you to correct. Reading and judging the results is up to you.
